About

I'm an embedded developer who likes working close to the hardware — mostly in C++. Supporting the projects with tools and tests in Python, and Rust. For a while now my attention has drifted toward machine learning, especially where it overlaps with biology.

I prefer small systems, they are easier to handle, and tend to carry bloat, that should be deleted. Away from the keyboard you'll usually find me learning or quietly keeping a side project alive.

Projects

  • rtwlib

    A small Rust library for tracing infrared rays as they are emitted, reflected, and accumulated across a scene.

    Sole author - once it was forked. Built as a personal tool for physically-grounded ray distibution experiments.

  • CAFA6-experimentation

    An open exploration of the CAFA6 protein-function prediction challenge, probing where model-assisted approaches genuinely help and where they don't.

    Solo research project; notebooks and notes tracking each experiment.

  • jacobi_allignment

    Alignment experiments that treat a system's internal values as directions in Jacobi space, aiming to make its incentives inspectable rather than implicit.

    Ongoing personal research.
