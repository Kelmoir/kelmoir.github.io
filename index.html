About

I'm an embedded developer who likes working close to the hardware, mostly in C++, with Python and Rust for the tooling and tests around it. Lately my attention has drifted toward machine learning, especially where it overlaps with biology.

I prefer small systems — they're easier to hold in your head, and easier to keep free of clutter. Away from the keyboard, you'll usually find me learning something new or quietly keeping a side project alive.

Projects

  • rtwlib

    A small Rust library for tracing infrared rays as they are emitted, reflected, and accumulated across a scene.

    Sole author, built as a personal tool for physically grounded ray-distribution experiments.

  • CAFA6-experimentation

    An open exploration of the CAFA6 protein-function prediction challenge, probing where model-assisted approaches genuinely help and where they don't.

    Solo research project; notebooks and notes tracking each experiment.

  • jacobi_alignment

    Alignment experiments that model a system's internal values as directions in Jacobi space, so its incentives can be inspected rather than inferred.

    Ongoing personal research.
